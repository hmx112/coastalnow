LOCAL SURF CONTEXT

Surf locations near Virginia Beach

1st Street Jetty

1st Street Jetty is a well-known Virginia Beach surf location close to Rudee Inlet. Local sandbanks and the jetty affect how waves break; a broader National Weather Service forecast is not an on-site measurement at this particular break.

Crowds, jetty structures, coastal currents and local restrictions require independent attention. Follow posted beach rules and alerts.

Coverage: CoastalNow uses NOAA/NWS area forecasts and rule-based scores, not real-time measurements at this surf break. No wave direction, board suitability or guaranteed safe surf is inferred.
